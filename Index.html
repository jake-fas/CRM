Privacy & terms

This private pilot stores your business records, contact details, activity notes, exclusions, and follow-up dates in the Google Sheet selected by the builder. Authorized Sheet editors and script editors can access those records. Google account and API services process live lookup requests.

Live Google Places content is displayed temporarily and is not saved by this app. Place IDs may be retained. Add permanent contact facts from your own interactions or sources with appropriate reuse rights. Do not treat a listed business phone as a direct manager number.

Nearby results are candidates, not an exhaustive business census. Opening hours and phone information may be incomplete or outdated. Respect do-not-contact instructions. This app is a private pilot with no affiliation or endorsement by Cintas or Google. Access and deletion requests should go to the person who owns your Sheet; they can remove your records and revoke access.
