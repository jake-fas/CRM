Fieldbook privacy policy

Fieldbook stores user-entered business labels, addresses, contact details, competitor observations, statuses, activities, notes, exclusion lists, and follow-up dates in the private Google Sheet chosen by the operator. Google place IDs may link records to live discovery. Authorized Sheet and script editors can access the saved records.

The app checks the signed-in Google account email against the operator's access list. Address and industry searches are sent to Google Maps Platform for geocoding and Places lookup. Google result payloads are displayed temporarily in the browser and are not saved by this app. The app does not use analytics cookies or browser local storage, sell your CRM data, or send reminder emails/push notifications.

Google services have their own processing and retention practices. The Google Privacy Policy is incorporated into this policy. External business websites opened from the app operate under their own privacy policies.

The operator retains CRM records until they are removed from the Sheet. Archiving hides a business from prospecting; it does not delete its history. The operator can revoke app/Sheet access or delete records on request, subject to Google backup/version-history behavior.
